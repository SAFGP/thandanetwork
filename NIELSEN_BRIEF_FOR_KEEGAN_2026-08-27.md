## Question 1. The Leading Hotels of the World membership, and what "private" means

He wrote: *"What part of Thanda is a member of Leading Hotels of the World? How does that fit with
the estate being completely private? Does 'private' in this sense actually mean 'privately owned'?
Ditto when properties are on site as rentals. Similarly Pierre saying in interview that there are
'no day visitors, no self-drive'. Am confused!"*

**He is confused because three different things are being carried by one word. Separate them and
it resolves.**

**Who holds the membership.** **Thanda Safari**, the safari hospitality business, listed by LHW as
"Thanda Safari, Hluhluwe, South Africa". **Not** The Royal Thanda Club, **not** the Royal Thanda
Residences. Thanda's own 26 page Royal Residences fact sheet contains the word "Leading" zero
times. So the accurate sentence is that the residences sit inside a reserve **whose safari
operation** is an LHW member.

**What the residences actually get.** A **service standard**, not a membership: hospitality and
concierge delivered to the Leading Quality Assurance benchmark.

**Why a commercial lodge and a private estate coexist.** Two rings.
- **Outer ring**: 16,500 hectares of fenced Big Five reserve, with four Thanda accommodations
  taking paying guests. This is where the LHW membership lives.
- **Inner ring**: the **386 hectare** Royal Thanda Club Estate, separately game fenced, no
  dangerous game, **44 full title freehold subdivisions**, an HOA and levies. This is the part
  that is private in the strict legal sense.

**"Private" should be used in three separate senses, never as one blanket word:**
- **Private in tenure**, at residence level. Full title freehold, individually owned. Our
  strongest and safest claim.
- **Private in access**, at reserve level. Fully fenced, in-house armed protection, no day
  visitors, no self-drive, no public access. Verifiable.
- **Private in ownership**, at reserve level. **This one needs a qualifier.** Thanda owns roughly
  **9,500 of the 16,500 hectares**. The balance, including the 6,700 hectare Mduna Royal Reserve
  that members traverse, is **leased from the Ingonyama Trust** on a 40 year term with a 40 year
  renewal, notarised onto each owner's title deed.

  **Do not let Forbes print "wholly privately owned reserve".** Accurate is: privately owned and
  operated, with long leasehold traversing over adjoining Zulu Royal trust land.

**Pierre's "no day visitors, no self-drive"** is an **access policy**, and it is entirely
consistent with a commercial lodge. It means nobody drives in for the afternoon and nobody drives
themselves around a Big Five reserve. It does not mean nobody stays. **Access is controlled;
ownership is separate. Conflating the two is exactly Nielsen's confusion.**

**One formulation that is true on every point, if it helps him write the line:**

> The residences are individually owned freehold homes on a 386 hectare access controlled estate
> at the centre of Thanda Safari, a fully fenced 16,500 hectare Big Five reserve with no day
> visitors, no self drive and no public access, whose safari operation is a member of The Leading
> Hotels of the World, and where owners may place their home into Thanda's managed rental
> programme when they are not in residence.

**CONFIRM with Pierre:**
- Is the LHW membership current as at August 2026?
- Are any Royal Residences bookable **inside** LHW inventory, or only through Thanda's own
  booking engine? We believe only the latter.
- Is "the LQA benchmark set by The Leading Hotels of the World" still correct 2026 phrasing?
  Pierre has said on record that LHW founded LQA but no longer owns it.

**Already actioned:** southafricafgp.com said the rental programme "forms part of The Leading
