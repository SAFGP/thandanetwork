## 1. LOGLINE

A man who has known one piece of Zulu land for fifty years explains why it cannot be replicated, and what it means to own a home on it.
Pierre Delvaux carries the whole film in his own words, the reserve, the villas and the club answer him in pictures.

---

## 2. HOW THIS FILM IS BUILT

- **Pierre drives it.** Every word of narration is Pierre Delvaux, verbatim, from the recorded interview. No voice over artist, no written script read to camera, no music-only montage.
- **Three messages in Keegan's order.** 1) The Land. 2) The Villas. 3) The Royal Thanda Club.
- **The buyer's arc runs underneath.** Helicopter in, over the reserve, game drive, the people, arrival at the villa, the fire, the table.
- **Rhythm.** Pierre soundbite, visual breath, Pierre soundbite, visual breath. Never two soundbites back to back without air between them.
- **Pierre is heard, not seen, until the last third.** Hold his face back. First reveal of Pierre on camera lands at 0:37 on "It's not about the real estate." That reveal is the turn from land to people.
- **No price anywhere.** Not spoken, not on screen, not implied.

## 8. NOTES FOR THE GRADE AND THE MIX

- **Grade.** Warm and dry through the land and the dancers, cooler and cleaner through the villa interiors, back to firelight warmth for the last 10 seconds. Do not crush the blacks in the night fire shots, the fire is the last image and it needs detail.
- **Music.** One piece, no edit points, no drop. It should thin out under every Pierre line and open up in the visual breaths. It must be fully resolved before the card, the card lands in near silence.
- **Pierre's audio.** He was recorded in Bert's bar with an audience present. Check lines 75 and 79 for room tone and applause bleed before committing to the reveal at 0:37. If Q3a is unusable, A3 on camera is the fallback reveal.
- **Subtitles.** If a subtitled social version is needed, type the corrected spellings, never the transcript spellings. Thanda, not Tundra.
