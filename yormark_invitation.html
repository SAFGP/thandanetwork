IV · The Family Stay

Designed around the three of you.

The pace is slow. The land is enormous. The detail is in the small things.

For Sophia

The Junior Rangers Course with Thanda's senior tracking team. An afternoon in the bush with Christian Sperka, the resident wildlife photographer. An outdoor cinema beneath the sacred Marula tree, and a helicopter day to Nine Mile Beach inside iSimangaliso, where the dolphins come close to shore.

For Michael & Dana

Twice-daily Big Five game drives with private guides. Bush breakfasts. A traditional Zulu ceremony hosted by a sangoma from the neighbouring community. Dinner at the Marula Tree, prepared by Thanda's executive chef. Stargazing with no ambient light for a hundred kilometres in any direction.

V · A Suggested Shape

Friday to Monday.

Friday
16 May · Arrival

Helicopter from King Shaka International, Durban. One hour to the reserve. Settle into Villa iZulu, the Olofsson family's private home. Sundowners and dinner under the stars.

Saturday
17 May · The Reserve

Sunrise game drive and bush breakfast. Junior Rangers programme for Sophia. Photography session with Christian Sperka. A walk-through of the Royal Residences in the late afternoon. Evening Zulu cultural ceremony, followed by dinner at the Marula Tree.

Sunday
18 May · The Coast

Helicopter to Nine Mile Beach inside iSimangaliso, the UNESCO coastal park. Beach braai. Return to the reserve mid-afternoon. Outdoor cinema beneath the Marula for Sophia.

Monday
19 May · Departure

Final sunrise game drive. Breakfast at the lodge. Helicopter departure to King Shaka by mid-morning, in time for onward travel.
