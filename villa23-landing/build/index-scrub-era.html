Turnkey Royal Residence

Traditional Induna, four bedrooms

This turnkey Royal Residence covers 661 square metres of total covered area, designed and furnished throughout around a unique Zulu theme. It is positioned for absolute privacy and uninterrupted views across the reserve.

Four en-suite bedroom pods radiate from a central living core, connected by shaded timber walkways to the pool deck, the boma and the sala beyond.

The Royal Thanda Club

A wilderness home, by invitation

The Royal Thanda Club at Thanda Safari is the Thanda Group's flagship. As a member you own a full title, freehold plot of protected land in what will become one of the most exclusive lifestyle estates in Africa.

Enjoy total privacy within the 386 hectare, access controlled Royal Thanda Club Estate, where only members and their guests are permitted, and where every facility and amenity is at hand.

Forty-four members

A legacy, the Royal way

The Royal Thanda Club is a private association of forty-four members, each with full-title ownership of land inside one of Africa's most protected wildernesses.

Rising from within Thanda Safari is the start of the Great Rift Valley, dating back over 130 million years. A land as ancient as the rolling hills King Shaka once walked, where our human ancestors lived for over 200,000 years in the neighbouring Lebombo caves, a site of international archaeological significance.
