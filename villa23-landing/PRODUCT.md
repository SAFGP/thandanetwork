# PRODUCT.md, Villa Mhlosinga listing page

register: brand

## What this is
A private listing page for Villa Mhlosinga, a Royal Residence at The Royal Thanda Club, Thanda Safari, KwaZulu-Natal. Presented by South Africa | Forbes Global Properties, the licensed South African member of Forbes Global Properties. Live at villamhlosinga.southafricafgp.com.

## Users
Ultra high net worth buyers and their advisors, invited or referred. They arrive from the main southafricafgp.com site, from WhatsApp links sent by the principal broker, or from the Forbes network. They read on iPhones and large desktops in equal measure. They have seen every luxury portal; they are impressed by restraint and bored by adjectives.

## Voice
Understated, certain, quiet. Access and selectivity over superlatives. Banned words: stunning, sumptuous, exquisite, breathtaking, showstopping. No dashes anywhere, use commas. The entity is always "South Africa | Forbes Global Properties", never reversed.

## Anti-references
Property24 and portal listing pages. Generic luxury templates with gold script fonts. Salesy CTAs ("Don't miss out"). Anything that reads as a brochure rather than a private document.

## Strategic principles
- The page is a private viewing, not an advert. One property, one narrative, one enquiry path.
- Photography carries the argument. Copy stays out of its way.
- Every listing page links back to the main site through the nav lockup and footer mark.
- The member disclaimer appears wherever the FGP mark appears.
