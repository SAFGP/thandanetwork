Thanda Residences are a serviced residential collection held by a small membership. Villa Mhlosinga is one of the Royal Residences of The Royal Thanda Club at Thanda Safari, the first and only Thanda Residences in Africa, where ownership comes by invitation.

03 · Turnkey Royal Residence
Turnkey Royal Residence

This turnkey Royal Residence covers 661 square metres of total covered area, designed and furnished throughout around a unique Zulu theme. It is positioned for absolute privacy and uninterrupted views across the reserve, sleeping eight in air-conditioned comfort, with living fully hosted by a dedicated executive chef, butler and host.

Four en-suite bedroom pods radiate from a central living core of lounge, dining, wine cellar and bar, connected by shaded timber walkways to the rim-flow pool, jacuzzi and children's pool, the boma and fire pit, and the yoga sala beyond.

04 · The Royal Thanda Club
The Royal Thanda Club

The Royal Thanda Club is an exclusive private club reserved for Royal Residence owners. Membership is by invitation only and is subject to the purchase of a property.

The Club brings together a select community of like-minded individuals who share a passion for conservation, luxury, and legacy.

06 · Forty-four members
Forty-four members

The Royal Thanda Club is a private association of forty-four members, each with full-title ownership of land inside one of Africa's most protected wildernesses.

Rising from within Thanda Safari is the start of the Great Rift Valley, dating back over 130 million years. A land as ancient as the rolling hills King Shaka once walked, where our human ancestors lived for over 200,000 years in the neighbouring Lebombo caves, a site of international archaeological significance.
