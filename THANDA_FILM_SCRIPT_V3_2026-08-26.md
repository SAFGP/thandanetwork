## 2. HOW THIS FILM IS BUILT

- **Pierre drives it.** Every word is Pierre Delvaux, verbatim from the recorded interview. No voice over artist, no script read to camera.
- **Three messages, in Keegan's order.** 1) The land. 2) **The villas, and that you can own one.** 3) The club, lightly.
- **The buyer's arc runs underneath.** Helicopter in, over the reserve, game drive, the people, arrival at the villa, living there, the fire, the table.
- **Rhythm.** Pierre soundbite, visual breath, Pierre soundbite, visual breath. Never two soundbites back to back without air.
- **Pierre is heard, not seen, until 0:37.** Hold his face back. The first reveal is the emotional turn.
- **No price anywhere.** Not spoken, not on screen, not implied.
- **No member count. No superlatives about conservation.**
